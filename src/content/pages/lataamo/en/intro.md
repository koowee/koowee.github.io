---
title: "Lataamo"
description: "Lataamo shows the nearest free EV chargers in Finland with their prices, and keeps your regular destinations one tap away."
---
Lataamo is an iPhone app for finding a free EV charger in Finland. Open it and you see the nearest chargers that are free right now, with their power and price, before you even get in the car.

- **Nearest free chargers.** A list of the closest chargers with a free point, filtered to the connectors your car uses. Fast charging and connectors are picked right at the top of the list and the map, so the filter is always in view.
- **Prices up front.** The price per kWh, per minute or per session, as the operator reports it.
- **Your destinations.** Save the places you drive to often and see at a glance how busy their chargers are.
- **Map.** Every charger on the map, coloured by whether it has a free point.
- **Long trips.** Enter a destination to see the chargers along the route and how much battery you'll arrive with. Lataamo suggests coffee and meal breaks by the clock at fast chargers with a café or restaurant nearby, and adds a charging stop when the battery wouldn't last otherwise. When you just want to get there fast, pick Fastest: Lataamo suggests charging stops only and roughly how long each charge takes.
- **Widget and Siri.** Check the chargers from your home screen, or ask Siri in Finnish.
- **Navigate.** One tap opens directions to the charger in Apple Maps or Google Maps.
- **Your car's charge (optional).** With your own Volvo or BMW / MINI developer credentials, Lataamo reads the battery level from the car.

Lataamo uses Finland's open charging data. No accounts, no ads and no tracking. The app is in Finnish.
