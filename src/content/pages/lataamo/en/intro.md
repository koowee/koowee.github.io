---
title: "Lataamo"
description: "Lataamo shows the nearest free EV chargers in Finland with their prices, and keeps your regular destinations one tap away."
---
Lataamo is an iPhone app for finding a free EV charger in Finland. Open it and you see the nearest chargers that are free right now, with their power and price, before you even get in the car.

- **Nearest free chargers.** A list of the closest chargers with a free point, filtered to the connectors your car uses. Fast charging only, if you like.
- **Prices up front.** The price per kWh, per minute or per session, as the operator reports it.
- **Your destinations.** Save the places you drive to often and see at a glance how busy their chargers are.
- **Widget and Siri.** Check the chargers from your home screen, or ask Siri in Finnish.
- **Navigate.** One tap opens directions to the charger in Apple Maps.

Lataamo uses Finland's open charging data. Your location stays on your phone. No accounts. The app is in Finnish.

Source of the charging data: Fintraffic / digitraffic.fi, license CC 4.0 BY.
