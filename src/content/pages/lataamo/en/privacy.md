## Summary

Lataamo has no accounts and no servers of its own. It uses your location on your iPhone to sort the chargers by distance. Your location only leaves the phone to Apple's map services, when the app works out driving distances or a route or searches for a place, and to Google Maps only if you open a route there yourself. We never see your data.

## Data the app handles

- Your location, while you use the app, if you allow it. It is used to find the nearest chargers and work out driving distances.
- Your settings, such as the connector types you use, the battery size, your break preferences and your saved destinations.
- If you sign in to Volvo: your own Volvo API keys and the sign-in tokens, kept in the iPhone's keychain, and your car's battery level and range.
- A copy of the public charger data, kept on your iPhone so the app also works with a weak connection.

## Where it is stored

Everything stays on your iPhone. The widget and Siri read the same data inside the phone. Lataamo sends your data to no servers of its own, because there are none.

## Third-party services

- **Digitraffic** (Fintraffic) provides the open charger data. The app downloads the same public lists for every user, and the requests contain no location or other information about you. As with any internet request, Fintraffic's servers see your IP address. Fintraffic's privacy policy applies to it.
- **Apple's map services** (MapKit) work out driving distances to chargers, the trip's route and the cafés and restaurants at break stops, and look up a place you type. These requests send Apple the route's start and end points, or the search text and the area near you. Apple Maps also opens when you ask for directions. Apple's privacy policy applies to them.
- **Google Maps** (optional): when you tap "Open in Google Maps" on a route, its destination and charging stops (and a start you typed) open in Google Maps for navigation. Lataamo sends Google nothing otherwise. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Maps.
- **Volvo Cars** (optional): if you sign in with Volvo ID, the app reads your car's battery data straight from Volvo's API. The sign-in returns to the app through this site (koowee.github.io/lataamo/volvo), which only passes it on and stores nothing. Volvo's privacy policy applies to Volvo ID and your car's data. You can sign out in Lataamo's Volvo settings.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Children

Lataamo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Your rights and contact

Because your data stays on your iPhone, you can change or delete it at any time. Deleting the app removes all of it. You can turn off location access in the iPhone's Settings. For any questions about privacy, email [valkama.app@gmail.com](mailto:valkama.app@gmail.com).

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
