## Summary

Lataamo has no accounts and no servers of its own. It uses your location on your iPhone to sort the chargers by distance, and your location is never sent anywhere. We never see your data.

## Data the app handles

- Your location, while you use the app, if you allow it. It is only used on your iPhone to find the nearest chargers.
- Your settings, such as the connector types you use, and your saved destinations.
- A copy of the public charger data, kept on your iPhone so the app also works with a weak connection.

## Where it is stored

Everything stays on your iPhone. Lataamo sends no data about you anywhere.

## Third-party services

- **Digitraffic** (Fintraffic) provides the open charger data. The app downloads the same public lists for every user, and the requests contain no location or other information about you. As with any internet request, Fintraffic's servers see your IP address. Fintraffic's privacy policy applies to it.
- **Apple Maps** opens when you ask for directions. Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Children

Lataamo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Your rights and contact

Because your data stays on your iPhone, you can change or delete it at any time. Deleting the app removes all of it. You can turn off location access in the iPhone's Settings. For any questions about privacy, email [valkama.app@gmail.com](mailto:valkama.app@gmail.com).

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
