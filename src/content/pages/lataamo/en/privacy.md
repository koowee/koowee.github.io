## Summary

Lataamo has no accounts and no servers of its own. It uses your location on your iPhone to sort the chargers by distance. Your location only leaves the phone to Apple's map services, when the app works out driving distances or a route or searches for a place, and to Google Maps only if you navigate with it yourself. We never see your data.

## Data the app handles

- Your location, while you use the app, if you allow it. It is used to find the nearest chargers and work out driving distances.
- Your settings, such as the connector types you use, the battery size, your break preferences and your saved destinations.
- If you sign in to Volvo: your own Volvo API keys, the sign-in tokens and your car's vehicle identification number (VIN), kept in the iPhone's keychain, and your car's battery level and range.
- If you sign in to BMW or MINI: your own CarData Client ID, the sign-in tokens, your car's VIN and the id of Lataamo's data container, kept in the iPhone's keychain, and your car's battery level, range and last read.
- A copy of the public charger data, kept on your iPhone so the app also works with a weak connection.

## Where it is stored

Everything stays on your iPhone. The widget and Siri read the same data inside the phone. Lataamo sends your data to no servers of its own, because there are none.

## Third-party services

- **Digitraffic** (Fintraffic) provides the open charger data. The app downloads the same public lists for every user, and the requests contain no location or other information about you. As with any internet request, Fintraffic's servers see your IP address. Fintraffic's privacy policy applies to it.
- **Apple's map services** (MapKit) work out driving distances to chargers, the trip's route and the cafés and restaurants at break stops, and look up a place you type or the address of a place you pick on the map. These requests send Apple the route's start and end points, the search text and the area near you, or the point picked on the map. Apple Maps also opens when you ask for directions. Apple's privacy policy applies to them.
- **Google Maps** (optional): if you choose Google Maps as the navigation app in the settings, the Navigate buttons and Siri open directions to the charger in Google Maps. When you tap "Open in Google Maps" on a route, its destination and charging stops (and a start you typed) open in Google Maps. Lataamo sends Google nothing otherwise. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Maps.
- **Volvo Cars** (optional): if you sign in with Volvo ID, the app reads your car's battery data straight from Volvo's API. The sign-in returns to the app through this site (koowee.github.io/lataamo/volvo), which only passes it on and stores nothing. Volvo's privacy policy applies to Volvo ID and your car's data. You can sign out in Lataamo's Volvo settings.
- **BMW / MINI** (optional): if you sign in to BMW CarData with your own Client ID, the app reads your car's battery data straight from BMW's API. You sign in with a code on BMW's own page. On its first read Lataamo creates a data container named "Lataamo" in your BMW account, which sets which car data the app may read (battery level, range and charging state). You can delete it on BMW's CarData page. BMW's privacy policy applies to your BMW account and your car's data. You can sign out in Lataamo's BMW / MINI settings.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Children

Lataamo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Your rights and contact

Because your data stays on your iPhone, you can change or delete it at any time. Deleting the app removes your settings and the charger data. iOS keeps keychain items after an app is deleted, so remove your car credentials with "Poista tunnukset" (Remove credentials) in Lataamo's Volvo or BMW / MINI settings. If you install Lataamo again later, it removes an earlier install's credentials on its first launch. You can turn off location access in the iPhone's Settings. For any questions about privacy, email [valkama.app@gmail.com](mailto:valkama.app@gmail.com).

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
