## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Where does the charger data come from?</summary>

From Fintraffic's open charging data at digitraffic.fi, which charging operators in Finland are required to publish. Lataamo shows it as it is: if an operator reports a charger late or wrongly, the app shows the same.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is a charger missing?</summary>

Lataamo shows every charger that its operator has reported to the open data. Not every operator has reported all of its stations yet. If a charger you know is missing, ask the operator to report it to Fintraffic.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How up to date is "free"?</summary>

Lataamo fetches the charger statuses about once a minute while the app is open. Many operators only report a change, so an old timestamp often just means nothing has changed. If a free charger's status is older than half an hour, the app says so.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is a price missing or different from the operator's app?</summary>

Not every operator publishes its prices in the open data. The price shown is the one for paying without a membership; member prices in the operator's own app can be lower.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How are breaks and charging stops worked out on a long trip?</summary>

Type a destination in the map's search. With Breaks, Lataamo suggests coffee and meal breaks at the times you choose in the settings, at fast chargers with a café or restaurant nearby, and adds a charging stop when the battery wouldn't last otherwise. With Fastest, it looks for the charging stops that get you there earliest and favours chargers with a café nearby. Charging times are estimates based on the battery size and the top charging power in the settings.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I open the route in Google Maps?</summary>

Yes. Once the route is worked out, tap "Open in Google Maps" in the route details. Google Maps gets the destination and the suggested charging stops as waypoints in the same order (up to nine), and navigation also works in CarPlay. If you start from your current location, Google Maps starts from where the phone is at that moment. To have the chargers' Navigate button and Siri open Google Maps too, choose Google Maps as the navigation app in the settings.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where does the battery level come from?</summary>

If you sign in to Volvo, Lataamo reads the level and range from the car. Otherwise you enter the starting charge in the route details, and Lataamo estimates the use along the way. The Volvo sign-in needs your own Volvo developer keys and is an optional extra. The [guide](/lataamo/guide/) explains how to get them.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I ask Siri?</summary>

Siri works in Finnish. Say "Lähin vapaa laturi Lataamo" and Siri tells you the nearest free charger by road. For a group of favourites, say "Lataamo onko vapaata"; Siri asks "Mikä ryhmä?" and you answer with the group's name. To look up a station, say "Etsi asema Lataamo".
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why doesn't the widget update right away?</summary>

iOS decides when widgets refresh, usually about every 15 minutes. The widget shows how old its data is. When it can't get a location of its own, it uses the one the app last used, for up to a week.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I join the beta?</summary>

Lataamo is in open beta before its App Store release. Install the free [TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664) app from the App Store on your iPhone, then open the [beta link](https://testflight.apple.com/join/rGHTGfPe) on that iPhone. Updates to the test version arrive through TestFlight. To send feedback, take a screenshot in the app and choose Share Beta Feedback, or send an email to the address below.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need an account?</summary>

No. Lataamo has no accounts. The only sign-in is the optional Volvo ID for the car's battery data.
</details>

</details>
