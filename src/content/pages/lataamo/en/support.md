## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Where does the charger data come from?</summary>

From Fintraffic's open charging data at digitraffic.fi, which charging operators in Finland are required to publish. Lataamo shows it as it is: if an operator reports a charger late or wrongly, the app shows the same.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How up to date is "free"?</summary>

Lataamo fetches the charger statuses about once a minute while the app is open. Many operators only report a change, so an old timestamp often just means nothing has changed. If a free charger's status is older than half an hour, the app says so.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is a price missing or different from the operator's app?</summary>

Not every operator publishes its prices in the open data. The price shown is the one for paying without a membership; member prices in the operator's own app can be lower.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need an account?</summary>

No. Lataamo has no accounts and no sign-in.
</details>

<summary>How do I contact you?</summary>

</details>
