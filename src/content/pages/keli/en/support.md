## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>How do I add my own places?</summary>

Open the Garmin Connect IQ app on your phone, find Keli under your apps and open its settings. Give each place a display name and either a place name (for example "Levi, Kittilä") or coordinates. You can add up to five places.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why does a place say it was not found?</summary>

Keli looks the name up in Open-Meteo's place search. Add the municipality after a comma, such as "Levi, Kittilä", or enter coordinates instead.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where does the weather data come from?</summary>

From Open-Meteo.com, licensed CC BY 4.0. Keli fetches it through your phone, so the phone needs a connection and the Garmin Connect app running.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Which watches does Keli work on?</summary>

First the Garmin fenix 8 Pro. More models will be added later.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I contact you?</summary>

Send an email to the address below. Please mention your watch model and software version if something is not working.
</details>
