## Summary

Keli has no accounts and no servers of its own. To get a forecast, it sends the coordinates of your places and your current location to the weather service. We never see your data.

## Data the app handles

- Your watch's location, if you allow it, to show the weather where you are.
- The places you save in the Connect IQ app settings.
- The latest forecasts, kept on your watch so it can show them without a connection.

## Where it is stored

On your watch, and your saved places in Garmin's Connect IQ app settings. Keli sends no data about you to us.

## Third-party services

Requests go from your watch through your phone's Garmin Connect app.

- **Open-Meteo** provides the forecasts and turns place names into coordinates. The requests contain coordinates or a place name, nothing else about you. As with any internet request, Open-Meteo's servers see your IP address. Open-Meteo's privacy policy applies to it.
- **BigDataCloud** turns your current location into a place name. The request contains only the coordinates. BigDataCloud's privacy policy applies to it.
- **Garmin** handles the connection between watch and phone and stores the app settings. Garmin's privacy policy applies to it.

The app contains no advertising or analytics.

## Children

Keli is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Your rights and contact

You can change or delete your places at any time in the Connect IQ app. Removing the app from your watch removes its data. For any questions about privacy, email [valkama.app@gmail.com](mailto:valkama.app@gmail.com).

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
