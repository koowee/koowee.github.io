---
Keli is a free weather app for Garmin watches, guided by a moose of the same name. It shows the weather where you are and at the places you care about, with the place name, wind and units always on screen.

- **Your places.** Save up to five places in the Connect IQ app on your phone, by name or by coordinates. Your current location is always on the list too.
- **Wind first.** Wind speed in m/s and its direction, right in the glance and on the main screen.
- **Now and in three hours.** Temperature, feels-like, weather and wind now, and what it will be like in three hours.
- **Hours and days.** The next twelve hours, then today and the next couple of days with highs, lows, wind and rain.
- **Glance.** Check the weather at your current location straight from the watch's glance list.

Keli is coming first to the Garmin fenix 8 Pro, with more watches to follow. The app is in English and Finnish.
