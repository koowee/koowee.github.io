## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Which sizes can my child choose?</summary>

4, 9, 16, 25, 36 or 49 pieces. On iPhone the biggest sizes are not shown, because the pieces would get too small for little fingers. In the parent section you can hide sizes that are too easy or too hard.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is a half-built puzzle saved?</summary>

Yes. Every puzzle the child has started is saved automatically and continues exactly where it was left, even if the app is closed.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What does the purchase unlock?</summary>

One theme is free. A single one-time purchase unlocks every theme, now and in later updates. The purchase is made in the parent section, behind a check that small children can't pass. If you reinstall the app or use another device, choose "Restore purchase" there.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I contact you?</summary>

Send an email to the address below. Please mention your device and system version if something is not working.
</details>
