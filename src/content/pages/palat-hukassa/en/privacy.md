## Summary

Palat Hukassa collects no data. It works fully offline and has no accounts, advertising or analytics.

## Data the app handles

Only the child's progress in the app: puzzles in progress, finished pictures and the parent's settings.

## Where it is stored

Only on the device. The app does not connect to the internet and sends nothing anywhere.

## Purchases

The in-app purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple ID.

## Third-party services

None. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party services.

## Children

Palat Hukassa is made for children aged 3 to 6. Because it collects no personal data and does not use the internet, no data about the child ever leaves the device. Settings, the purchase and the only outside link are behind the parent check.

## Your rights and contact

Because your data stays on your device, you can change or delete it in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of it. For any questions about privacy, email [valkama.app@gmail.com](mailto:valkama.app@gmail.com).

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
