Palat Hukassa is a jigsaw puzzle game for children aged 3 to 6, made for iPad and working on iPhone too. The child picks a theme, a picture and how many pieces to build, and puts the picture together from pieces scattered around the board.

- **Many themes.** Animals, landscapes, hobbies, vehicles, space and the sea, each with several pictures.
- **From 4 to 49 pieces.** Four big pieces for the youngest, up to 49 for a six-year-old. A parent can hide the sizes that don't fit their child.
- **Real jigsaw pieces.** Knobs and holes, a gentle snap when a piece is near its place, and pieces lying all over the table like a real puzzle.
- **No reading needed.** Everything the child uses is pictures and icons.
- **Carries on where you left off.** Puzzles are saved automatically, and any puzzle can be started over.

No ads, no accounts and no internet connection needed. A free taster theme, and one in-app purchase unlocks every theme.
