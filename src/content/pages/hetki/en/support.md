## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>The subdials show "–". Why?</summary>

Hetki has not fetched the weather yet, or the latest weather is over 6 hours old. The first update can take up to an hour after you choose the face. The phone needs a connection and the Garmin Connect app running. Weather older than 2 hours is shown in grey.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Which way does the wind arrow point?</summary>

Where the wind blows to, like the arrow in Keli. The big number is the speed and the number in brackets the gusts, both in m/s.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What does the line at the bottom show?</summary>

One thing at a time: thunder within 6 hours, rain ending, rain starting within 2 hours, otherwise the next sunrise or sunset. In Norway, Sweden, Finland and Denmark the rain times come from weather radar to the minute; elsewhere to the hour.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I change the colour?</summary>

Open the Garmin Connect IQ app on your phone, find Hetki under your watch faces and open its settings.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Which watches does Hetki work on?</summary>

The Garmin fenix 7 Pro family. More models may follow.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I contact you?</summary>

Send an email to the address below. Please mention your watch model and software version if something is not working.
</details>
