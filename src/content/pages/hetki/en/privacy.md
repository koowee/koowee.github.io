## Summary

Hetki has no accounts and no servers of its own. To get the weather, it sends your current location to the weather services. We never see your data.

## Data the face handles

- Your watch's last known location, or the location your phone gave the watch's weather, to fetch the weather where you are.
- The latest weather, kept on your watch so the face can show it without a connection.
- Your chosen colour, in the Connect IQ app settings.

## Third-party services

Requests go from your watch through your phone's Garmin Connect app, once an hour.

- **Open-Meteo** provides the forecast and the sunrise and sunset times. The request contains your coordinates, nothing else about you. As with any internet request, Open-Meteo's servers see your IP address. Open-Meteo's privacy policy applies to it.
- **MET Norway** (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute) provides the radar rain forecast in the Nordic countries. The request contains your coordinates and the app name, nothing else about you; MET Norway's servers see your IP address. MET Norway's privacy policy applies to it.
- **Garmin** handles the connection between watch and phone and stores the face's settings. Garmin's privacy policy applies to it.

The face contains no advertising or analytics.

## Children

Hetki is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Your rights and contact

Removing the face from your watch removes its data. For any questions about privacy, email [valkama.app@gmail.com](mailto:valkama.app@gmail.com).
