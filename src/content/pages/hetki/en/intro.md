title: "Hetki"
description: "Hetki is a free analog watch face for Garmin watches with the weather that matters: temperature, wind, chance of rain and when the rain starts."
---
Hetki is a free analog watch face for Garmin watches. It keeps a classic dial and puts the weather from [Keli](/keli/) in its subdials, so you see it every time you turn your wrist.

- **Weather and chance of rain.** Weather symbol and temperature; the ring around them fills with the chance of rain in the next 3 hours.
- **Wind.** An arrow shows where the wind blows, with speed and gusts in m/s.
- **One smart line.** Thunder coming, rain starting or ending (to the minute from weather radar in the Nordic countries), otherwise the next sunrise or sunset.
- **The basics.** Date, steps and battery in days.
- **Your colour.** Pick the ring colour in the Connect IQ app on your phone.

Hetki updates the weather once an hour at your location through your phone. It is made for the Garmin fenix 7 Pro family, in English and Finnish.
