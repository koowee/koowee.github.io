## Getting started

1. Search for a place, use your current location or pick one of the suggested aurora spots.
2. Star the places you want to follow. Favourites are checked for notifications.
3. Allow notifications to hear when tonight looks good. You can change the limit in Settings.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>How is the estimate calculated?</summary>

The estimate combines NOAA's Kp forecast and live readings from NOAA and the Finnish Meteorological Institute with the cloud forecast, darkness and moonlight at your place. It is an estimate, not a promise: auroras can surprise in both directions. The [guide](/revontulet/guide/#how-the-estimate-is-computed) explains it in more detail.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why did I not get a notification?</summary>

Notifications are sent only for favourites, and only when the best hour of tonight reaches your limit (50 % by default). Check that notifications are allowed for Revontulet in iOS Settings. The Favourites tab says so at the top if they are off.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is the sky camera image missing?</summary>

The image comes from the nearest Finnish Meteorological Institute all-sky camera, in Kilpisjärvi, Kevo, Muonio or Helsinki, within 300 km. Places further away have no camera image. When the latest image is more than an hour old, a short note is shown instead.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need an account?</summary>

No. Revontulet has no accounts and no sign-in.
</details>
