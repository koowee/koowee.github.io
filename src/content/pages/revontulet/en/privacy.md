## Summary

Revontulet has no accounts and no servers of our own. Your places and settings stay on your iPhone, and the app only fetches open forecast data.

## Data the app handles

- The places you search for or save as favourites, and your settings.
- Your location, only if you choose "use current location". iOS asks for permission first, and the app uses it only while you use it.
- The optional sighting log, if you turn it on: whether you saw auroras, with the estimate at that moment.

## Where it is stored

Everything is stored on your device, shared only with the app's own widgets. Nothing is synced to iCloud or sent to us. A CSV export of the sighting log goes only where you choose to share it.

## Third-party services

To make the forecast, the app requests open data directly from these services:

- **Finnish Meteorological Institute open data**, for the cloud forecast. The request contains the place's coordinates rounded to about one kilometre, as any weather request does. See FMI's [privacy notice](https://en.ilmatieteenlaitos.fi/data-protection).
- **NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center** and the **Finnish Meteorological Institute**, for space weather, the Kp forecast and sky camera images. These requests contain no information about you or your place.
- **Apple Maps**, when you search for a place or use your current location (to find the place's name). Apple's privacy policy applies.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Children

Revontulet is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Your rights and contact

Because your data stays on your device, you can view, change and delete it in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its data. For any questions about privacy, email [valkama.app@gmail.com](mailto:valkama.app@gmail.com).

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
