---
title: "Repovahti"
description: "Repovahti estimates hour by hour how likely you are to see the northern lights at your chosen places, and tells you when tonight looks good."
---
Repovahti is an aurora forecast for iPhone. It combines space weather, cloud cover, darkness and the moon into one simple estimate: how likely you are to see the northern lights, hour by hour, at the places you care about.

- **Tonight at a glance.** The best hour, the good window and the chance for every hour of the next 24.
- **Notifications for favourites.** Star your places and get a heads-up when tonight looks good.
- **Right now.** Real-time solar wind and the latest image from the nearest all-sky camera.
- **Widgets.** Your favourites tonight, or one place's night as an hourly chart, on the home screen and lock screen.

No accounts and no servers of our own: the app fetches open data straight from its sources. The app is in English and Finnish.
