## Summary

Hilpertti collects no data at all. It works fully offline and has no accounts, advertising, analytics or purchases.

## Data the app handles

Only the child's progress in the app: stars, unlocked friends, coloured pictures and the parent settings.

## Where it is stored

On the device only. The app does not connect to the internet and never sends anything by itself. In the parent section, behind the parental gate, a parent can choose to share a coloured picture with the iOS share sheet.

## Third-party services

None. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party services. The parent section has a link to the licence of the app's artwork, and it is behind the parental gate.

## Children

Hilpertti is made for children of about three years and up. Because it collects no personal data and has no network access, no data about a child ever leaves the device. Settings and the only external link are behind a parental gate.

## Your rights and contact

Because your data stays on your device, you can view, change and delete it in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its data. For any questions about privacy, email [valkama.app@gmail.com](mailto:valkama.app@gmail.com).

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
