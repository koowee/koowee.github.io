## For parents

Hilpertti is ready to play as soon as it opens. To change settings, open the parent section: hold the small gear on the home screen for three seconds, then answer a short sum.

In the parent section you can change the language, turn sound effects, music and speech on or off, set a daily play-time limit, choose the game levels, look at and share your child's coloured pictures, and reset the progress.

The [guide](/hilpertti/guide/) describes every game and every setting in the parent section.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Does Hilpertti need an internet connection?</summary>

No. Hilpertti works fully offline and never connects to the internet.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How does the play-time limit work?</summary>

When the daily limit is reached, Hilpertti shows a good-night screen. You can change the limit in the parent section.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Hilpertti doesn't speak. What can I do?</summary>

First check that the device's volume is up and that speech is on in the parent section. If the device has no speech voice for the chosen language, the parent section says so and shows where to add one: **Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices**.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Which languages does Hilpertti speak?</summary>

Finnish, English and Swedish. On the first launch Hilpertti follows the device language, and you can change it in the parent section. The letters in the Draw game follow the language.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why did the level change by itself?</summary>

The level goes up when your child gets at least four of a round's five tasks right on the first try, and down when two or fewer go right first time. You can set the level yourself in the parent section.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I start over?</summary>

Open the parent section and choose reset to clear the child's progress.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Are there in-app purchases or ads?</summary>

No. There are no purchases, ads or links that a child could open by accident.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I contact you?</summary>

Send an email to the address below. Please mention your device and system version if something is not working.
</details>
