This guide is for parents. The child doesn't need it: Hilpertti says every task aloud, and if the child gets stuck, the right spot starts to glow after a moment.

## Home screen

The home screen shows Hilpertti the reindeer, the friends your child has collected and the game cards.
- **Tap a card** to hear the game's name; the game opens a moment later. A second tap opens it at once.
- **Tap Hilpertti or a friend** to hear its name and see it dance.
- **The small gear** in the corner opens the parent section: hold it for three seconds and answer a sum.

## Stars and friends

Every game is a round of five tasks, and each finished task gives a star. Every fifth star brings a gift box on the way home, with a new animal friend inside. There are six friends: bunny, fox, bear, penguin, hedgehog and frog. The next friend waits on the home screen as a silhouette, showing how many stars are still to go.

## Levels

Each game has four levels. The level goes up by itself when your child gets at least four of a round's five tasks right on the first try, and down when two or fewer go right first time. You can set each game's level yourself in the parent section.

A wrong answer is never punished: Hilpertti helps kindly and the task can be tried again. If the child thinks for a long time or answers wrong a few times, the right spot starts to glow.

## The games

- **Maze.** The child leads an animal with a finger through a hedge maze to its treat. The maze grows from 4 × 4 to 7 × 7.
- **Colours.** Things are sorted into baskets of the same colour. Higher levels have more colours and close shades, like red and orange.
- **Numbers.** "How many ducks?" The child can tap the animals while Hilpertti counts them aloud, then picks the right number.
- **Odd one out.** Find the one picture that differs: first another thing, then another colour, and finally a small detail.
- **Colouring.** Colour Hilpertti, the collected friends or six landscapes with a brush and an eraser in 16 colours. A picture is kept, so the child can come back to it.
- **Strings.** Follow a tangled string with a finger from an animal to its food.
- **Differences.** Find the differences between two pictures, two to five of them.
- **Pairs.** Take each animal to its pair: its baby, its food or its home. Every other round has the farm animals.
- **Memory.** Turn two cards at a time and find the pairs, 6 to 12 cards.
- **Sounds.** "Who says this?" The child taps the animal that makes the sound, like a cow, a pig or a duck.
- **Draw.** Trace a big letter or number with a finger, stroke by stroke. A finished letter is named with a word and a picture, and a number gives that many stars. The letters follow the app's language.
- **Puzzle.** Real jigsaw pieces, 4 to 12 of them. On levels 1–2 the finished picture shows faintly where the pieces go.
- **Balloons.** "Pop number five!" Pop the right balloon. The numbers grow by level from up to nine to up to twenty.
- **Repeat.** The friends hop and play their notes one after another, and the child taps them in the same order. A right sequence grows by one friend at a time.

## Parent section

Hold the small gear on the home screen for three seconds and answer a short sum. After three wrong answers the gate stays closed for a minute.

- **Language.** Finnish, English or Swedish. On the first launch the app follows the device language.
- **Sounds.** Sound effects, music and speech can each be turned off. If the device has no speech voice for the chosen language, the section says where to add one.
- **Play time.** A daily play-time limit (10 to 60 minutes, or no limit) and the time played today. When the time is up, Hilpertti says good night after the task in hand. The time starts again every day.
- **Levels.** Each game's level, and whether the puzzle shows its finished picture under the pieces.
- **Coloured pictures.** The pictures your child has coloured. Open one to share or save it.
- **Reset progress.** Stars, friends and levels start over. Settings and coloured pictures are kept.
- **Credits.** The licence of the speech synthesiser voice.
