---
title: "Hilpertti"
description: "Hilpertti the reindeer guides small children through short Finnish learning games for numbers, colours and finger control."
---
Hilpertti is a Finnish learning app for children from about three years old, for iPad and iPhone. Hilpertti the reindeer guides short, calm games, and everything is spoken, so the child does not need to read.

- **Games that teach.** Counting, colour sorting, a hedge-garden maze, colouring and finding the odd one out, each on several levels.
- **Friends to collect.** Stars unlock animal friends who dance with Hilpertti on the home screen.
- **Made for parents too.** A parent section behind a gate sets sounds, a daily play-time limit and game levels.
- **Safe by design.** No network, no accounts, no ads and no purchases.

The app is in Finnish.
