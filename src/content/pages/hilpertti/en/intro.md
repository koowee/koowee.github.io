title: "Hilpertti"
description: "Hilpertti the reindeer guides small children through 14 short learning games for numbers, letters, colours, memory and finger control."
---
Hilpertti is a learning app for children from about three years old, for iPad and iPhone, in Finnish, English and Swedish. Hilpertti the reindeer guides short, calm games, and everything is spoken, so the child does not need to read.

- **14 games that teach.** Counting, numbers and letters, colours, memory, spot the difference, jigsaw puzzles, colouring, animal sounds and more. Each has four levels, and the level follows the child by itself.
- **Friends to collect.** Stars unlock animal friends who dance with Hilpertti on the home screen.
- **Made for parents too.** A parent section behind a gate sets the language, sounds, a daily play-time limit and game levels. The [guide](/hilpertti/guide/) describes every game.
- **Safe by design.** No network, no accounts, no ads and no purchases.

The app follows the device language, and a parent can change it.
