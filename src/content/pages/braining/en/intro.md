---
title: "Braining"
description: "Braining is a voice notebook: record a thought on your iPhone or iPad, and it becomes a note, transcribed locally on your iPhone or on your Mac."
---
Braining is a voice notebook for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Speak a thought into your iPhone, iPad or Mac, and Braining turns it into a note that shows up on all your devices.

- **Record right away.** Start recording from the lock screen, Control Centre, the Action button, the home screen widget or Siri. Recording keeps going when the phone locks.
- **Transcribed on your own device.** Braining turns speech into text locally with WhisperKit, in Finnish. On newer iPhones and iPads you can turn on transcription in the app, and the device transcribes its own recordings once it has downloaded a 627 MB speech model over Wi-Fi. Otherwise the Mac app transcribes them. Your recordings are not sent to a transcription service.
- **Notes on every device.** Notes sync through your own iCloud, and you can write and edit them offline too. Export them as Markdown files whenever you like.
- **Organise and find.** Add categories, tags, due dates and reminders. Search and filters find the note you need.

No accounts and no Braining servers. The app is in Finnish.
