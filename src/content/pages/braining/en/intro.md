title: "Braining"
description: "Braining is a voice notebook: record a thought on your iPhone or iPad, and your Mac transcribes it locally into a note."
---
Braining is a voice notebook for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Speak a thought into your iPhone or iPad, and Braining transcribes it on your Mac into a note that shows up on all your devices.

- **Record right away.** Start recording from the lock screen, Control Centre, the Action button, the home screen widget or Siri. Recording keeps going when the phone locks.
- **Transcribed on your Mac.** The Braining Mac app turns speech into text locally with WhisperKit, in Finnish. Your recordings are not sent to a transcription service.
- **Notes on every device.** Notes sync through your own iCloud, and you can write and edit them offline too. Export them as Markdown files whenever you like.
- **Organise and find.** Add categories, tags, due dates and reminders. Search and filters find the note you need.

No accounts and no Braining servers. The app is in Finnish.
