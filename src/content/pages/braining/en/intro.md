---
title: "Braining"
description: "Braining turns voice notes into Markdown notes: record on your iPhone or iPad, and your Mac transcribes them locally."
---
Braining is a voice notebook for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Speak a thought into your iPhone or iPad, and Braining transcribes it on your Mac into a plain Markdown note in your own iCloud Drive folder.

- **Record in one tap.** Start recording from the app or straight from the home screen widget.
- **Transcribed on your Mac.** Speech is turned into text locally with WhisperKit, in Finnish. Your recordings are not sent to a transcription service.
- **Plain Markdown files.** Every note is an ordinary Markdown file in a folder you choose, so you can open it in any other app too.
- **Organise and find.** Edit notes on any of your devices, and add categories, tags, due dates and reminders. Search and filters find the note you need.

No accounts and no Braining servers. The app is in Finnish.
