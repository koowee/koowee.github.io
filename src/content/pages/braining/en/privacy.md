## Summary

Braining has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your recordings and notes are files in an iCloud Drive folder you choose, and your Mac does the transcription. We never see your data.

## Data the app handles

- Voice recordings you make on your iPhone or iPad. The iPhone and iPad ask for microphone access only to record them.
- Your notes: title, text, category, tags, due date and reminder.
- Your list of categories.

Reminders use notifications on your device, and the app asks for permission to send them the first time you save a reminder.

## Where it is stored

A recording stays in the app on your iPhone or iPad until it has been copied to the folder you chose. Recordings, notes and the category list are stored in that folder. If the folder is in iCloud Drive, Apple stores and syncs the files under its own terms, and Braining adds no encryption of its own. Once the Mac has saved the note, it removes the recording from the folder.

## Third-party services

- **Transcription** runs on your Mac with WhisperKit. Your recordings are not sent anywhere to be transcribed.
- The first time it transcribes, the Mac downloads the speech model from the internet. No recordings or notes are sent with that download.
- **iCloud Drive** syncs the folder you chose between your devices. Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Children

Braining is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Your rights and contact

Because your data stays on your devices and in your own folder, you can view, change and delete it at any time. Deleting the app removes the recordings that are still waiting in it. Recordings and notes in your own folder stay there until you delete them. For any questions about privacy, email [valkama.app@gmail.com](mailto:valkama.app@gmail.com).

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
