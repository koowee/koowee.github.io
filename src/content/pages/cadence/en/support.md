## Getting started

1. Add your first bike with **+** and pick its type. Cadence fills in typical parts and service intervals, which you can edit.
2. To count wear automatically, connect Strava under **Asetukset → Strava**, or allow Cadence to read cycling workouts from Apple Health.
3. When you service or replace a part, open it and choose **Merkitse huolletuksi** or **Vaihda uuteen**.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Why does Strava need my own API keys?</summary>

Cadence connects to Strava with an API application that you create in your own Strava account. That way your rides go straight from Strava to your phone, without any server of ours in between. Asetukset → Strava shows the steps.
</details>

<details>
<summary>My rides do not wear any parts. What is wrong?</summary>

A ride wears parts only when it is linked to a Cadence bike. Link your Strava bikes to Cadence bikes after a sync or on the bike page. Indoor rides, such as Zwift, wear nothing unless you pick a bike for them.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I move my data to a new phone?</summary>

Use **Asetukset → Tee varmuuskopio** to save one backup file to Files or iCloud Drive, and **Tuo varmuuskopio** on the new phone. The backup does not include your Strava or Bosch keys, so connect those again.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I connect my Bosch eBike?</summary>

Yes. Under Asetukset → Bosch eBike you can connect Bosch's Data Act service with your own Client-ID. The bike page then shows the odometer, motor hours and battery information.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is Cadence available in English?</summary>

Not yet. The app is in Finnish for now.
</details>
