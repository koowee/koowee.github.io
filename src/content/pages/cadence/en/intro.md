---
title: "Cadence"
description: "Cadence keeps track of your bikes, their parts and when each part needs service, using your rides from Strava or Apple Health."
---
Cadence is a bike maintenance app for iPhone and iPad. It knows what is on each of your bikes, how far and how long every part has been ridden, and when it is time for a service.

- **Parts and service intervals.** Add a bike from a template for its type and get its parts with suggested intervals in kilometres, ride hours or months.
- **Wear from your rides.** Rides from Strava or Apple Health wear the parts on the bike you rode, and a reminder arrives before a part is due.
- **Service diary with receipts.** Log services and replacements, scan receipts with the camera and see what each bike really costs per kilometre.
- **Widgets.** See the next services on your home screen or lock screen.

The app is in Finnish.
