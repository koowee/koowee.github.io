## Summary

Cadence keeps all your data on your own iPhone or iPad. We do not run servers, we do not collect analytics and we never see your data.

## Data the app handles

- Your bikes, parts, service intervals, service diary entries and costs, which you enter yourself.
- Photos of bikes and receipts that you take with the camera or pick from Photos. Receipts are stored as small greyscale images or PDFs.
- Rides: distance, time, climbing, the bike used and the start location of each ride (used to show the town name), from Strava or Apple Health. A ride's route, heart rate and power are read from Strava or Apple Health only when you open the ride and are not stored.
- If you connect a Bosch eBike: the odometer, motor hours and battery information that Bosch provides.

## Where it is stored

Everything is stored on your device. Cadence does not sync to iCloud. If you make a backup, the backup file goes where you choose to save it, such as Files or iCloud Drive. Your Strava and Bosch keys and sign-in tokens are kept in the device's Keychain and are never included in backups.

## Third-party services

- **Strava.** If you connect Strava, the app talks directly to Strava's API using an API application you create in your own Strava account. Strava's [privacy policy](https://www.strava.com/legal/privacy) applies to your Strava account.
- **Bosch eBike.** If you connect a Bosch eBike, the app reads data from Bosch's Data Act API with your own Client-ID. Bosch's privacy terms apply to your Bosch account.
- **Apple Health.** If you allow it, Cadence reads cycling workouts to count part wear. It does not write to Apple Health, and Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.
- **Apple's geocoding service.** To show the town name of a ride, the ride's start coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service. Apple's privacy policy applies.
- **Links.** Some buttons open web pages, such as Strava or a bike geometry site. Those pages are subject to their own policies.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Children

Cadence is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Your rights and contact

Because your data stays on your device, you can view, change and delete it in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its data. Backup files you saved to your own folder stay there until you delete them. If you disconnect Strava or Bosch, the app stops fetching data from them. For any questions about privacy, email [valkama.apps@gmail.com](mailto:valkama.apps@gmail.com).

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
