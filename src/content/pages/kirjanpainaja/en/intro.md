---
title: "Kirjanpainaja"
description: "Kirjanpainaja is a Mac app for the bookkeeping of a Finnish sole proprietorship and forestry, with a companion app for iPhone and iPad."
---
Kirjanpainaja is bookkeeping for a Finnish sole proprietorship (toiminimi) and forestry, for the Mac. It turns your entries and receipts into the figures you need for the VAT return and tax forms 2C and 5, in the same order as OmaVero.

- **Entries with VAT.** Each entry carries its VAT treatment and links to its receipt in your own synced folder.
- **Tax returns, ready to copy.** The VAT return and forms 2C and 5, with every figure one click from your clipboard.
- **Year lock.** Checks before locking, a frozen copy of the figures and unlocking only with a written reason.
- **iPhone and iPad companion.** Scan receipts on the go and see the year's figures read-only. On the iPad, entries and tax returns show the list and the details side by side.

Your books stay on your own devices and in your own folder. The app is in Finnish.

Kirjanpainaja is not tax or accounting advice. Check the figures before you file.
