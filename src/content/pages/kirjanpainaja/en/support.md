The [guide](/kirjanpainaja/guide/) covers every part of the app. It is a summary of the Mac app's own guide, under **Ohje → Kirjanpainajan ohje** (Help → Kirjanpainaja guide).

## Getting started

1. On the Mac, choose a storage folder under **Asetukset → Yleiset**. Any synced folder works, such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive, or a local folder. Receipts are kept there.
2. Choose your line of business, which sets the starting categories.
3. Add entries, attach receipts and check the figures under **Veroilmoitukset**.

To use the iPhone or iPad app, choose the same storage folder in Files. Receipts you scan go into the folder's *Kirjanpainaja/Saapuneet*, and the Mac books them as usual.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Where is my bookkeeping stored?</summary>

The database is stored in the app's own container on your Mac, and receipts are in your storage folder. Backups of the database go to *Kirjanpainaja/Varmuuskopiot* in the same folder; see Asetukset → Varmuuskopiot.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why does the iPhone or iPad app not show my latest figures?</summary>

The mobile app shows a snapshot that the Mac writes under Asetukset → Yleiset → Mobiilisovellus. Update it on the Mac, wait for your folder to sync, then pull down to reload in the mobile app. Dropbox, Google Drive and OneDrive can show the phone an older copy. In that case choose an iCloud Drive mobile folder for the figures, see the [guide](/kirjanpainaja/guide/#iphone-and-ipad).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does Kirjanpainaja send anything to the tax authority?</summary>

No. The app calculates the figures and you enter them in OmaVero yourself. Always check the figures before filing.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I change a locked year?</summary>

Only by unlocking it with a written reason. The reason and the earlier figures are kept.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I import my old bookkeeping from another program?</summary>

You can import past years as history on the Import page with Tuo vanha vuosi taulukosta… (Import an earlier year from a spreadsheet). Export the old bookkeeping from the program as a CSV or Excel file. History appears in Analysis for comparison, but has no tax returns. See the [guide](/en/kirjanpainaja/guide/#getting-started).
</details>

<details>
<summary>My bank's statement is not recognised. What do I do?</summary>

In the window that opens, choose which column is the date, the amount and the other details, and the account the statement belongs to. Your choices are remembered for the next statement from the same bank.
</details>

</details>
