## Summary

Kirjanpainaja keeps your bookkeeping on your own Mac and in a folder you choose. We do not run servers, we do not collect analytics and we never see your data.

## Data the app handles

- Your bookkeeping: entries, categories, trips, machine hours, depreciation and year settings.
- Receipts you attach, scan or photograph.
- On the iPhone: receipts you scan or pick, and a read-only snapshot of the figures that your Mac writes.

Receipt text is read on the device, and receipts are never sent anywhere to be read. The iPhone asks for camera access only to scan receipts.

## Where it is stored

The bookkeeping database is stored in the app's own container on your Mac. Receipts, backups and the iPhone snapshot are stored in the storage folder you choose. If that folder is synced by a service such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive, that service stores and syncs the files under its own terms. The Mac app itself does not connect to the internet.

## Third-party services

None are built into the app. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. The only service involved is the folder sync you choose yourself.

## Children

Kirjanpainaja is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Your rights and contact

Because your data stays on your device, you can view, change and delete it in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its data. Receipts, backups and the iPhone snapshot in your own storage folder stay there until you delete them. For any questions about privacy, email [valkama.app@gmail.com](mailto:valkama.app@gmail.com).

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
