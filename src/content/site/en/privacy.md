This page covers the website you are reading. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from the app's page.

## No cookies, no analytics

The site uses no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party scripts or fonts. It does not ask for or store any information about you.

## Hosting

The site is static and hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address to keep the service secure. That logging is covered by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), and we have no access to it.

## Email

If you send an email, we use your address and message only to reply to you.
